  [P] Sanjeevani: AI Platform for Verifiable Himalayan Health
  [P] @Sanjeevani-TeleHealth                                                                                                                                           2
  [P] Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
  [P] An autonomous, dialect-aware tele-triage and clinical decision platform that digitizes and secures primary healthcare delivery across remote mountain terrains.
  [P] It ensures diagnostic accuracy, cultural safety, and real-time visibility through Manchester Triage protocols, AYUSH safety guardrails, and AI analytics.
  [P] •  Autonomous clinical triage app designed for high-altitude villages and ASHA workers to operate in rugged, low-connectivity terrains.
  [P] •  Conversational Garhwali dialect engine with auto-phonetic normalization and sub-50ms streaming Indic voice synthesis.
  [P] •  Evidence-based AYUSH & CCRAS integration providing verified classical home remedies backed by an in-memory Safety Matrix blocking contraindications.
  [P] •  Non-invasive Computer Vision screening detecting palpebral conjunctiva anemia, scleral jaundice, and oral leukoplakia with Gray-World lighting correction.
  [P] •  Offline-first architecture with SQLite WAL enabling complete offline triage with background auto-sync upon network availability.
  [P] •  Supervisor and CMO epidemiological dashboard providing live village health heatmaps, outbreak alerts, and FHIR standard referral summaries.
  [P] How it addresses the problem
  [P] •  Eliminates healthcare distance barriers in steep terrain where villagers hike 15–30 km to reach Primary Health Centers.
  [P] •  Enables instant emergency escalation classifying Red-tier conditions (stroke, cardiac crisis) in <2s and initiating 1-tap 108 SOS dispatch.
  [P] •  Prevents tertiary hospital overcrowding by resolving ~45% of mild routine symptoms safely with verified AYUSH home care.
  [P] •  Overcomes low literacy and language barriers via hands-free Regional/Hindi voice interaction with zero medical jargon.
  [P] •  Guarantees healthcare continuity during disasters functioning reliably during monsoons, landslides, and optical fiber breaks.
  [P] Innovation and Uniqueness
  [P] •  Deterministic Safety Knowledge Graph programmatically vetoes hazardous herb-comorbidity interactions (licorice in hypertension, ginger in ulcers).
  [P] •  Gray-World Adaptive White Balance CV normalizes extreme Himalayan lighting disparities (glacier UV glare vs dim stone huts).
  [P] •  Dual-Layer Offline Triage Pipeline combines client-side local rule decision trees with central LangGraph cloud intelligence.
  [P] •  Sub-50ms Pre-Cached Dialect Audio delivers immediate spoken responses even on degraded 2G mobile data connections.
  [P] •  HL7 FHIR v4.0.1 Interoperability generates standardized electronic referral records ready for direct ABDM ingest.

  [P] @Sanjeevani-TeleHealth                                                                                                                                           3
  [P] Tech Stack for Mobile App and Web
  [P] Modular, High-Performance Stack
  [P] • Client Application:
PWA (React 18, Vite), Tailwind CSS, MediaPipe FaceMesh/Pose, Web Workers
  [P] • Backend API Core:
FastAPI (Async Python 3.13), LangGraph Multi-Agent, SQLAlchemy, Uvicorn
  [P] • AI & Speech Engine:
Sarvam AI (Indic STT & Bulbul TTS), Gemini Flash / Sarvam-2B, ChromaDB RAG
  [P] • Database & Caching:
SQLite in WAL mode (thread-safe concurrent sync), In-Memory Audio LRU Cache
  [P] • CV Pipeline:
OpenCV, NumPy, Gray-World White Balance, CIELAB color thresholding, CLAHE
  [P] • Standards:
HL7 FHIR Release 4, ABDM Electronic Health Record standards, Zero-PII Audit Log
  [P] System Architecture & Prototype Flow
  [P] Seamless Pipeline: Multimodal Edge Input → Multi-Agent Triage → Verified Clinical Output
  [P] 1. Multimodal Edge Capture  |  Patient speaks in Garhwali or Hindi, or ASHA uploads camera scan
  [P] • Sarvam Indic STT converts audio into normalized Devanagari text.• Dialect transliteration engine decodes Romanized Garhwali idioms.• Optical camera module captures palpebral conjunctiva/sclera under Gray World white-balance.
  [P] 2. LangGraph Agentic Triage  |  Stateful Multi-Agent Clinical Evaluation
  [P] • Node 1 (Triage Evaluator): Executes Manchester Triage Matrix & ESI severity scoring.• Node 2 (Safety Guard): Checks patient comorbidities against Safety Knowledge Graph.• Node 3 (Remedy Matcher): ChromaDB vector search retrieves verified CCRAS home remedies.
  [P] 3. Clinical Verification & Output  |  Strict Deterministic Delivery Protocol
  [P] • Green Tier: Safe AYUSH preparation steps + dosage + lifestyle wellness guidance.• Yellow Tier: Scheduled 24h Primary Health Center tele-referral with doctor notification.• Red Tier: Immediate high-priority siren, 108 ambulance dispatch, GPS facility mapping.
  [P] 4. Sync & Government Integration  |  Offline Persistence & National ABDM Standard
  [P] • In offline mode, client-side decision trees provide uninterrupted emergency triage.• SQLite WAL mode processes concurrent batched sync from ASHA field workers.• Formats FHIR JSON clinical encounter documents for state health authorities.
